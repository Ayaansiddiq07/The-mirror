# The Mirror — opening video prompt and storyboard

## Generation prompt

Create a restrained, premium 30-second 16:9 product-launch film for a satirical smartphone app called **THE MIRROR**. Black-and-white palette only; tactile photographic realism; high contrast, soft silver highlights, deep blacks, subtle film grain, deliberate camera movement, editorial pacing, quiet luxury. Begin with the first human reflection on gently rippling water. Travel through a poetic visual timeline—water, polished metal, a hand mirror, a glass vanity mirror, then a modern smartphone screen—using elegant match cuts and restrained dissolves. At 00:20, the phone floats in a dark studio. Show a carefully controlled exploded product visualization: screen glass, display, camera, and rear shell drift apart by a few centimeters in a slow, precise, layered separation, then align again. This is an engineered product visualization, not an explosion, impact, shattering, or destructive effect. At 00:25 reveal the exact title **THE MIRROR** in crisp white typography, centered on black, and hold through 00:30. No extra text, logos, fake UI, spoken narration, loud audio, rapid flashing, or strobing. Motion should be smooth and accessible, with no luminance flashes. Keep safe margins for projection.

## Production notes

- Deliver 1920×1080, 16:9, 30 seconds, 24 or 30 fps, with no baked-in subtitles.
- Keep monochrome grade, restrained contrast, and motion that remains comfortable to watch.
- Use a custom-rendered generic phone; avoid manufacturer marks or confusing the app with a real phone brand.
- Keep the separated parts close and aligned. This is a product visualization, not an explosion.
- The live website includes a local vector fallback for the opening visual if the optional Unsplash photograph or web fonts cannot load.
