# The Mirror — Feature Voice-over Cue Map

The 41 matching MP3 clips are in `music-clips/` and are wired to the listed controls, events, and Keynote quotes. This document is the recording and playback reference. The Gold, delete, Kudla, and full keynote tracks are separate assets documented in [INTERACTION_VOICEOVER.md](INTERACTION_VOICEOVER.md) and [KEYNOTE_VOICEOVER.md](KEYNOTE_VOICEOVER.md).

## ElevenLabs setup

- Voice: the same relaxed Indian male voice used for the existing Kudla clips.
- Delivery: conversational Hindi/Hinglish, lightly Mangalorean/Kudla flavour, understated and dry. Say “saar” naturally; do not imitate or exaggerate an accent.
- Pace: medium, with a short deadpan pause before the punchline. No music, ambience, or sound effects.
- Record every numbered cue in order as one continuous track. Do not speak cue numbers, filenames, section headings, or bracketed delivery notes. Leave about 1.2 seconds of clean silence between cues and 2 seconds at the end. Send the original untrimmed file back; it can be split into the named clips below.
- Keep each clip short. These are optional product jokes, so the existing interface must stay usable with audio muted or unavailable.
