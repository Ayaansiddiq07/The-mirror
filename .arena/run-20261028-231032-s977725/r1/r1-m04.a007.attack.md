ATTACK 1 [MAJOR] Don’t claim the prototype is offline without verifying it
Where: “Call it an offline browser prototype” and the sample answer: “We built an offline browser prototype…”
Problem: The task materials establish a presentation site and a temporary local camera preview, but do not establish that the app works offline. Those facts do not imply offline operation; for example, if the browser needs a network connection to load the app, the proposed line is false and undermines the trust the review is trying to protect. Make the wording conditional on a verified offline build, or call it a browser prototype and state only the supported privacy facts.
