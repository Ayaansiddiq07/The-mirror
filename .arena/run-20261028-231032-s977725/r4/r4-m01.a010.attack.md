ATTACK 1 [MAJOR] Camera stop claim misses a pending-permission race
Where: “the implementation context says camera tracks stop on slide changes, when the page is hidden, and on unload.”
Problem: This is not reliable for a concrete edge case in the supplied demo: click Enable live mirror, leave the demo slide while the browser's camera-permission request is still pending, then grant permission. The slide-change handler calls stopCamera while cameraStream is still null; enableCamera later assigns the returned stream, leaving the camera active after navigation. The review should flag this race and avoid telling the team to “confirm the camera stops on exit” as if current handlers guarantee it; recommend rechecking the current slide/visibility after getUserMedia resolves and immediately stopping a stale stream.
