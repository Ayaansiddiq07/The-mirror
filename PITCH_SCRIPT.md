# The Mirror — three-minute pitch script

**Total: 3:00, including the silent 30-second opening film.** Keep the opening silent and let the image do the work. Four team members speak once each after it. Speaker labels can be replaced with names.

| Time | Speaker | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:30 | Speaker 1 (silent) | Water reflection → mirror timeline → layered phone reveal → THE MIRROR title |
| 00:30–01:00 | Speaker 1 | Product reveal |
| 01:00–01:45 | Speaker 2 | Silver/Gold live demo |
| 01:45–02:25 | Speaker 3 | Satirical pricing |
| 02:25–03:00 | Speaker 4 | What we built, why it is useless, close |

## 00:00–00:30 — Speaker 1, silent film

No narration. Start on a first reflection in water. Move through a brisk visual history: water, polished metal, glass, then the smartphone. Resolve the phone into a layered product visualization (the pieces separate gently; no explosion effect), and reveal **THE MIRROR** at 00:25. Keep the soundtrack silent or use restrained room tone only.

## 00:30–01:00 — Speaker 1, product reveal

“Introducing The Mirror. It turns the front-facing camera into an elegant, purpose-built reflection experience. It is instant, ultra-clear, and powered by the same camera that was already on your phone. We have engineered every detail, including the glass, which is conveniently the screen you already own.”

## 01:00–01:45 — Speaker 2, live demo

“Here is our live demo. Silver gives your reflection a tasteful, partial-screen window, leaving the rest of your display available for… nothing. Now we switch to Gold. The reflection expands to the entire screen. Same camera. Same person. More screen. Camera permission is optional; the preview stays on this device. We never record or upload it.”

**On screen:** Toggle Silver, then Gold. If camera permission is denied or unavailable, keep the local illustration visible and continue. The live preview is temporary, local, and never recorded or uploaded.

## 01:45–02:25 — Speaker 3, satirical pricing

“Silver is free: half a screen, all the reflection. Gold is a mock, one-time ₹2,000 upgrade for the rest of your screen. For ₹5,000, the extras bundle is pure imagination: a confidence filter, unlimited angles—which is still one angle—and a complimentary cloth. The 25 GB download and 10 GB updates every two months? Also fictional. None of these buttons charges money or downloads files. Our margins are extraordinary because our costs are zero.”

**On screen:** The 25 GB download and 10 GB updates every two months may appear as fictional product claims in mock UI. They do not trigger downloads. Any ₹2,000 deletion fee is a dismissible joke; leaving remains free.

## 02:25–03:00 — Speaker 4, close

“We built a browser demo of a front-camera mirror with Silver and Gold modes. It is useless because your phone already has the camera and screen to do the same thing; we simply sell the empty space as a premium upgrade. There is a fake ₹2,000 deletion fee, which you can dismiss. Leaving is free. We made a mirror for a phone that already had one. That is the point of useless software. Thank you.”

**Pace note:** The spoken sections are written for a brisk, clear delivery with pauses during the interface toggles. If rehearsal runs long, trim the feature lists first. Keep the one privacy line, the clear mock-pricing/download disclaimer, and the concise explanation of what the team built and why it is useless. The one-time first-run preview is an assumption for the separate app prompt; do not claim it is implemented in this browser presentation.
