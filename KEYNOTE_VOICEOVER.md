Generate one calm, deadpan narration. Aim for about 105 words per minute and leave a brief pause at each slide cue. Do not read the time labels or bracketed cues aloud.

Welcome to The Mirror: a twenty-five-gigabyte download that shows you your own face. The trial is five hundred dollars. Tap Pay and the receipt says zero. It is a demo checkout; the only thing charged is the room’s patience.

### 0:50 · Silver and Gold

### 1:25 · Updates
