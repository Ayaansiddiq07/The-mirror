ATTACK 1 [MINOR] Room tone conflicts with the required silent opening
Where: In the script section “00:00–00:30 — Speaker 1, silent film”: “Keep the soundtrack silent or use restrained room tone only.”
Problem: The assignment fixes this slot as a silent 30-second opening film. “Room tone” is audible audio, so a team following that option would no longer have a silent film. Remove the room-tone option and specify no audio for the full 30 seconds.
