ATTACK 1 [MAJOR] It skips the required open-questions-first resolution
Where: The strategy card says, “List every open question the task leaves. Resolve each one with reasoning or a stated assumption, then answer.” The response starts with a premise assessment and ranked objections, then lists assumptions at the end.
Problem: This reverses the required workflow and does not identify the unresolved questions before making recommendations (for example, whether the live implementation actually matches the script’s privacy claims and whether the film lands the reveal on time). The later scope assumption acknowledges those unknowns but does not resolve them or state them as open questions up front, as instructed.
